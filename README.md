## Executive Summary

This repository hosts the academic portfolio of **Dr. Sanoj Kumar**, Senior Associate Professor in the Data Science Cluster, SOCS, UPES Dehradun. The portfolio highlights teaching, research, publications, projects, awards, and professional service in applied mathematics, data science, optimization, digital image processing, computer vision, machine learning, and deep learning.

## Research Interests

- Mathematical Statistics, Numerical Analysis, and Optimization Techniques
- Digital Image Processing, Computer Vision, and Machine Learning
- Deep Learning for medical imaging and brain tumor segmentation
- Image watermarking, reconstruction, texture classification, and visual surveillance
- AI-driven mathematical modeling and healthcare applications
