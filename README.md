## Executive Summary

This repository contains the academic portfolio website of **Dr. Sanoj Kumar**, Senior Associate Professor in the Data Science Cluster, SOCS, UPES Dehradun. The portfolio presents his teaching, research, publications, funded/project activity, awards, academic service, and professional profile in applied mathematics, data science, computer vision, machine learning, optimization, and digital image processing.

## Current Position

- **Senior Associate Professor**, Data Science Cluster, SOCS, UPES Dehradun
- Former **Associate Professor** and **Assistant Professor (Selection Grade)** at UPES
- Former **Postdoctoral Fellow**, Department of Mathematics and Computer Science, University of Udine, Italy
- Former **Teaching Assistant and MHRD Research Fellow**, Department of Mathematics, IIT Roorkee
- Former **Guest Lecturer**, Department of Mathematics, MNNIT Allahabad
